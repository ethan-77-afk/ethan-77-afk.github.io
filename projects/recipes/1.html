tips and tricks + substitutions

  • tips/tricks
    • Use room temperature butter
    • Cool down the dough before starting
    • Dont over mix your ingredients
    • Use parchment paper in the oven
    • Check your oven temperature
  • Substitutions
    • You can use coconut oil instead of butter
    • You can use unsweetened applesauce instead of eggs
    • You can use brown sugar instead of normal sugar
  1. Preheat to 375 F
  2. mix the flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl
  3. beat the butter, w hite sugar, and brown sugar together
  4. mix in the eggs and vanilla extract
  5. stir everything together
  6. mold them into little baking balls
  7. bake for 10 minutes
  8. commit severe gluttony when theyre done
Ingredient Amount
Flour 2 1/4 cupts
Baking soda 1 teaspoon
Salt 1 teaspoon
Butter 1 cup
white sugar 3/4 cup
brown sugar 3/4 cup
Vanillia extract 1 teaspoon
Eggs 2 large
Chocolate chips 2 cups

Total preparation time: roughly 30 minutes Servings: 25 medium sized cookies
