tips and tricks + substitutions

  • tips/tricks
    • Use room temperature butter
    • Cool down the dough before starting
    • Dont over mix your ingredients
    • Use parchment paper in the oven
    • Check your oven temperature
  • Substitutions
    • You can use coconut oil instead of butter
    • You can use unsweetened applesauce instead of eggs
    • You can use brown sugar instead of normal sugar
Ingredient Amount Directions
Flour 2 1/4 cupts Mix with baking soda and salt
Baking soda 1 teaspoon Mix with flour and salt
Salt 1 teaspoon Mix with flour and baking soda.
Butter 1 cup Beat with white and brown sugar
white sugar 3/4 cup Beat with butter and brown sugar
brown sugar 3/4 cup Beat with butter and white sugar
Vanillia extract 1 teaspoon Mix in with the eggs
Eggs 2 large Mix with the butter, sugars, and vanilla
Chocolate chips 2 cups Stir into the finished dough

After using all these ingredients, Bake in the oven at 375 degrees Fahreinheit for 10 minutes and your all set

Total preparation time: roughly 30 minutes Servings: 25 medium sized cookies
