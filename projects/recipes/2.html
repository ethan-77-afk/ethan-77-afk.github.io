My magnificent brownies

I chose this brownie recipe because it has been used in my family since my grandparents started making brownies. We used to make these alongside cookies every week, and were my personal favorite I swear they put some kind of addictive substance in the batter -my dad


tips and tricks + substitutions

  • tips/tricks
    • Don't overmix the batter
    • Use good quality chocolate for better brownies
    • Grease the baking dish before you put stuff in
    • Preheat your oven
  • Substitutions
    • You can use coconut oil instead of butter
    • You can use dark chocolate instead of milk chocolate if your a health guru
    • You can also add extra stuff like nuts or a banana
`

    instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F
  2. Mix the melted butter and sugar, then add eggs and vanilla
  3. stir in the flour cacao powder and salt
  4. spread the batter into a greased baking dish
  5. bake for 25 minutes
  6. enjoy your brownies and dont burn yourself like a moron

    7. total prep time, 30 minutes

    serving: 9 brownies

    made by me

Ingredient Amount
Flour 1/2 cup
cacao powder 1/3 cup
Salt 1/4 teaspoon
Butter 1 cup
sugar 1 cup
Eggs 1 large
